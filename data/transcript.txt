Physician: Hello, Mr. Khan. How are you feeling today?

Patient: Hello, doctor. Much better than last week. The cough has eased, but I still feel a bit weak.

Physician: I’m glad to hear the cough has improved. Let’s go back a little—when did your symptoms first start?

Patient: About ten days ago. It started with a sore throat and mild fever, then within two days I developed a bad cough and chest pain when breathing deeply.

Physician: Did you experience any shortness of breath?

Patient: Yes, especially when walking around. I felt breathless after climbing even one flight of stairs.

Physician: And when you first came in, we diagnosed you with a chest infection and started antibiotics, correct?

Patient: Yes, I was given a five-day course of amoxicillin and some cough syrup.

Physician: Did you complete the full course of antibiotics?

Patient: Yes, I finished them two days ago. The fever went away after the second day, and the chest pain has reduced a lot.

Physician: That’s good. How about your cough? Is it still bringing up phlegm?

Patient: A little, but it’s mostly clear now—not thick like before.

Physician: Any wheezing or night sweats?

Patient: No, none.

Physician: Alright. Let’s check your chest and lungs with a stethoscope.

[Physical Examination Conducted]

Physician: Your lungs sound clear now, with only a few mild crackles at the base. No wheezing or signs of pneumonia. Oxygen levels are normal too.

Patient: That’s reassuring, doctor.

Physician: Yes, your infection has almost completely resolved. The weakness is common—it may take another week or two before you feel fully energetic again. Just make sure to rest, drink plenty of fluids, and continue with light activity as you regain strength.

Patient: Should I stay off work a bit longer?

Physician: If your job is physically demanding, I’d suggest another three to four days of rest. If it’s desk-based, you can return as soon as you feel ready.

Patient: Alright, I’ll take it easy for a few more days.

Physician: Good. If you develop high fever again, worsening cough, or severe breathlessness, come back immediately. But otherwise, I don’t see any reason you won’t make a full recovery.

Patient: Thank you, doctor. I really appreciate your help.

Physician: You’re welcome, Mr. Khan. Take care, and I’ll see you again if needed.
