Physician: Can you describe how the injury happened?

Patient: I was running down the field, tried to turn quickly, and suddenly felt a sharp pain in my knee. I couldn’t put weight on it at all afterward.

Physician: That must have been very painful. Did you notice any swelling immediately?

Patient: Yes, within an hour my knee was swollen and I couldn’t bend it properly.

Physician: After surgery, how was your recovery in the first few weeks?

Patient: The first two weeks were tough. I was on crutches, had pain, and couldn’t sleep well. But after I started physiotherapy, things gradually got better.

Physician: And how many physiotherapy sessions have you had so far?

Patient: I’ve completed twelve sessions. They’ve really helped with strengthening and flexibility.

Physician: That’s good progress. Do you still feel pain during normal activities, like walking or climbing stairs?

Patient: Walking is fine now, but stairs can sometimes cause mild pain. It’s not severe, just uncomfortable.

Physician: Have you been able to return to sports or any physical hobbies?

Patient: Not yet. I’ve been cautious. I only do light jogging under the physiotherapist’s guidance.

Physician: That’s wise. How about daily life—work, driving, routine activities?

Patient: I’m back at work full-time, and driving is fine. It’s mostly just the stairs and squatting that are still difficult.

Physician: Alright, let’s do a physical examination to check your knee’s stability and mobility.
